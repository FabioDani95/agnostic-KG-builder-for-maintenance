# RoboLift RL-5 Field Notes

Document type: service bulletin
Language: English
Asset: RoboLift RL-5

## Page 1 - Inspection

Check that the arm is clear before applying power.

## Page 2 - Fault Table

| Code | Operator report | Probable cause | Service instruction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E17 | arm jerks then stops | loose encoder cable at joint J2 | re-seat J2 encoder cable and tighten both connector screws |
| E22 | gripper will not close | low pneumatic pressure below 5 bar | restore air supply to 6 bar and repeat grip test |

## Page 3 - Split Text Addendum

If E17 returns after re-seating the cable, inspect the encoder harness for
damage. Replace the harness only when the cable jacket is cut or crushed.

For E22, do not replace the gripper before checking the air regulator.
