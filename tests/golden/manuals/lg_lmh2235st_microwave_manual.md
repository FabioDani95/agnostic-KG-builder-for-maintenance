## Page 11 - General Information for Service

GENERAL PRECAUTIONS IN USE.
Never operate the unit when it is empty. Operating the oven with no load may
shorten the life of the magnetron.

TRIAL OPERATION. After installation, the following sequences and results
should be checked carefully. Put a container filled with water (about 1 liter)
into the oven, and close the door tightly. Set cooking time for 10 minutes.
Press the START button. Make sure the cavity light comes on. After about
5 minutes, make sure the primary interlock switch, the secondary interlock
switch and the interlock monitor switch operate properly by opening and
closing the door several times. Confirm the water is hot. Finally, measure the
output power according to the POWER OUTPUT MEASUREMENT section.

The safety systems incorporated in this model are: primary interlock switch,
secondary interlock switch, interlock monitor switch, choke system, oven
cavity thermostat.

## Page 12 - Self Diagnosis and Error Code

SAFETY CAUTION. Since nearly 4,000 volts exists in some circuits of this unit,
repairs should be carried out with great care. Never plug the unit into a
power source to determine which component is defective in the high voltage
section. To avoid possible exposure to microwave energy leakage, make sure the
primary interlock switch, the secondary interlock switch and the interlock
monitor switch operate properly by opening and closing the door several times
before servicing. Check microwave energy leakage must be below the limit of
5 mW/cm2.

6-3. Self diagnosis (humidity sensor and PCB thermistor check) and Error code.

Press "Defrost weight/time" for 2 seconds, the unit will display "TEST" and
beep once. The unit will check if the PCB humidity sensor and PCB thermistor
are short or open. If it has an error, it will beep and display an error code.
If the unit is normal, it will beep and display "PASS". Press "Clear" to
return to Standby mode.

Error code F-1. Symptom: PCB thermistor short.
Error code F-2. Symptom: PCB thermistor open.
Error code F-4. Symptom: Humidity sensor open or short.

## Page 15 - No Display or Dead

6-4. Troubleshooting. No Display or Dead.

Step 1. When you push any key, is there any beeping sound? Does the display
operate? If no, power off and continue.
Step 2. Is the connector connected to the PCB? If no, reconnect or repair the
connector.
Step 3. Is there any beeping sound in the continuity test between the power
cord and the connector? If no, check the power cord. If yes, go to step 4.
Step 4. Is there any beeping sound in the continuity test between the ends of
the fuse? If no, the fuse has no continuity (open fuse): replace the fuse.
Step 5. Is there any beeping sound in the continuity test between the ends of
the noise filter? If no, the noise filter has no continuity: replace the
noise filter.
Step 6. Otherwise, check the PCB and replace the PCB (EBR75341201).

## Page 22 - Turntable Motor Does Not Work

6-4. Troubleshooting. Turntable Motor Does Not Work flow chart.
Power ON.
Step 1. Does the turntable motor operate?
Step 2. When door is closed, does the oven lamp turn on? If no, adjust the

## Page 23 - Turns On Automatically

6-4. Troubleshooting. Turns On Automatically flow chart.
Step 1. Repeat door open and close at least three times. Does the product
turn on automatically without pushing the start key?
Step 2. Is there any beeping sound in the continuity test between the ends of
the latch board secondary switch? (Door should be closed.) If no, adjust the
latch board (refer to section 9-1, 9-2).
Step 3. When the door is closed, does the oven lamp turn on?
Step 4. When the door is opened, is the voltage of the door sensing part over
4 V? (Pin 11 to Pin 12.) If no, replace the PCB (EBR75341201).
Step 5. Is the voltage of both ends of the D37 over 8 V? If no, replace the
PCB (EBR75341201). If yes, the PCB has no problem.

## Page 24 - Microwave Leakage Test

CAUTION. Be sure to check microwave leakage prior to servicing the unit.
Microwave leakage measurement with the outer case removed: when you replace
the magnetron, measure for leakage before the outer case is installed and
after repair is completed. Leakage must be below the limit of 5 mW/cm2.
