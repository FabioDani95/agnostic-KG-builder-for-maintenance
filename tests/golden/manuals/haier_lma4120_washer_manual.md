## Page 16 - Trouble Alarm And Solve Method

If there is a fault or the machine does not operate correctly, remove the power
plug before checking the machine.

Problem: The machine does not drain or drains slowly. The display shows E1.
Possible cause: Is the drain hose blocked?
What to do: Remove the blockage. Push the Start/Pause button once.

Problem: The machine stops running. The display shows E2.
Possible cause: The top lid is not closed.
What to do: Close the top lid. Push the Start/Pause button once.

Problem: The machine does not spin. The display shows E3.
Possible causes: Has the laundry been placed unevenly in the machine? Is the
machine level?
What to do: Redistribute the load evenly. Push the Start/Pause button once.
Level the machine.

Problem: The display shows E4.
Possible causes: Is the water tap closed or has the water supply been
interrupted? Is the water inlet valve blocked? Is the water pressure too low?
Is the drain hose set too low?
What to do: Open the water tap. Clean the water inlet valve. Use after the
water pressure is normal. The height of the end of the drain hose should be
between 800 mm and 1200 mm.

Problem: The display shows FA.
Possible cause: The water level sensor has failed.
What to do: Please contact Customer Care.

Problem: The display shows F2.
Possible cause: Is the water level too high?
What to do: Please contact Customer Care.

Problem: The display shows EL.
Possible cause: Is the laundry too much?
What to do: Put part of the laundry out, then push the Start/Pause button.

Note: when the digital tube displays the abnormal symbol during working, it
buzzes once every two minutes, then the power will be cut automatically after
ten minutes.

## Page 18 - No Water Filling And No Rotation In Washing

6-2-2 No water filling.

Switch on the power. Push the Start/Pause button.

If there is action sound of the water inlet valve, remove the foreign materials
of the water inlet valve filter screen or raise the water pressure to
0.03 Mpa - 1 Mpa before use.

If there is no action sound and voltage exists at the two ends of the water
inlet valve, replace the water inlet valve.

If there is no voltage at the water inlet valve but voltage exists at the output
of the computer sequencer, replace the wire unit.

If there is no voltage at the computer sequencer output, replace the computer
sequencer.

6-2-3 No rotation in washing, or rotate to one direction only.

Start the test without water. Enter no-water washing.

If the motor acts, check the transmission system carefully. If it is normal,
check whether the bearing part is locking the clutch.

If voltage exists at the motor and the motor overheat protector acts, check the
reason.

If the capacitor has broken circuit or short circuit, replace the capacitor. If
not, replace the motor.

## Page 19 - No Draining And Keep Filling Water

6-2-4 No draining.

Set rinse or spin. Start the machine.

If the drain motor rolls up normally, check the drain system like the drain
valve.

If the drain motor does not act and voltage exists at the two ends of the drain
motor, replace the drain motor.

If no voltage exists at the drain motor but voltage exists at the output of the
computer sequencer, replace the wire unit.

If no voltage exists at the output of the computer sequencer, replace the
computer sequencer.

6-2-5 Keep filling water.

Open the water faucet without switching on the power.

If water can fill into the water inlet valve, replace the water inlet valve.

Switch on the power. Do not push the Start/Pause button.

If water can fill into the water inlet valve, replace the computer sequencer.

If the wire connecting the computer board to the water level sensor is bad,
replace the wire unit.

If the gas cell has water, it leaks gas. Please check and repair the gas guide
system.

If the gas guide system is blocked, clear foreign materials.

If the gas guide system is not blocked, replace the water level switch.
