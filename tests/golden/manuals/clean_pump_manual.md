# Acme Pump P-100 Service Manual

Document type: maintenance manual
Language: English
Asset: Acme Pump P-100

## 1. Safety

Disconnect power before opening the motor cover. Wear eye protection when
checking the seal chamber.

## 2. Troubleshooting

### Symptom: Pump does not start

Likely failure mode: blown motor fuse.

Corrective action: replace the 10 A motor fuse, close the fuse holder, and
restart the pump.

Evidence page: 2.

### Symptom: Low discharge pressure

Likely failure mode: clogged inlet filter.

Corrective action: remove the inlet filter, wash it with clean water, reinstall
it, and run a pressure test.

Evidence page: 2.

## 3. Maintenance Interval

Inspect the inlet filter every 250 operating hours. Replace the motor fuse only
after verifying that the supply voltage is correct.
