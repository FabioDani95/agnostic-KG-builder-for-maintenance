# Acme Pump P-100 Service Manual

Document type: maintenance manual
Language: English
Asset: Acme Pump P-100

## 1. Safety

Disconnect power before opening the motor cover. Wear eye protection when
checking the seal chamber.

The labels F3, CN-10, and REF-22 are wiring and connector references. They are
not display alarms and are not service fault codes.

## 2. Troubleshooting

### Symptom: Pump does not start

Likely failure mode: blown motor fuse.

Corrective action: replace the 10 A motor fuse, close the fuse holder, and
restart the pump.

Evidence page: 2.

### Symptom: Low discharge pressure

Likely failure mode: clogged inlet filter.

Corrective action: remove the inlet filter, wash it with clean water, reinstall
it, and run a pressure test.

Evidence page: 2.

## 3. Maintenance Interval

Inspect the inlet filter every 250 operating hours. Replace the motor fuse only
after verifying that the supply voltage is correct.

## 4. Installation Notes

The P-100 pump must be mounted on a level base. Use four M8 bolts and tighten
them evenly in a cross pattern. The orientation arrow on the casing must point
toward the discharge pipe.

The serial label may include strings such as P100-A12, F3, and CN-10. These are
manufacturing references and connector names, not operator-visible errors.

## 5. Preventive Maintenance Checklist

| Item | Interval | Instruction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motor cover | 500 hours | Check that the cover screws are present. |
| Seal chamber | 500 hours | Inspect for staining around the drain plug. |
| Coupling guard | 1000 hours | Verify that the guard is fitted before operation. |

These checklist rows are inspections only. They do not indicate a current fault
and they do not replace the troubleshooting procedure on page 2.

## 6. Spare Parts Reference

| Reference | Description | Quantity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F3 | 10 A motor fuse | 2 |
| CN-10 | Motor harness connector | 1 |
| KIT-22 | Inlet filter service kit | 1 |

Part references are printed on the service label. Do not report these references
as error codes.

## 7. Operator Notes

After maintenance, run the pump with clean water for two minutes. Confirm that
the discharge valve is open before returning the pump to production.

If the pump is stored for more than 90 days, rotate the shaft by hand once per
month. This storage step is preventive and is not a corrective action for either
of the page 2 symptoms.
