## Page 15 - Service Mode

For service technician use only.
All ADA 18" & 24" dishwashers are programmed with a service mode to aid the
technician in troubleshooting the dishwasher. Each component is cycled to
verify if it is functioning correctly.

To enter service mode (24" dishwasher, front display):
1. Turn the dishwasher off and make sure no LEDs are lit.
2. Disconnect power to the dishwasher.
3. Reapply power with the door open and quickly press and hold both the On/Off
and Start/Pause buttons within 60 seconds from connecting power. Close the
door and then the dishwasher will run as below.

Service mode chart:
1. WATER VALVE (SSD 05, turbidity value): turn on water valve. The flow meter
will allow 3 L of water to enter the dishwasher. This step will display the
turbidity value on the SSD.
2. WASH PUMP / HEATER (SSD 04): turn on the wash pump. After 10 seconds the
heater will turn on. The beeper will sound when the temperature has risen
3 - 5 degrees. The dishwasher will stop when the temperature has reached 57 C.
3. HIGH WASH PUMP / DISPENSER (SSD 03): turn on the wash pump for 8 seconds
after 2 second delay. Then turn on the dispenser for 45 seconds.
4. PAUSE (SSD 02): pause for 30 seconds.
5. DRAIN PUMP (SSD 01): turn on the drain pump for 30 seconds.
6. END (board type code): beep one time.
To exit service mode: disconnect power to the dishwasher.
SSD = Seven Segment Display.

## Page 18 - Service Error Codes

For service technician use only.
DANGER. Electrical shock hazard. Only authorized technicians should perform
diagnostic voltage measurements. After performing voltage measurements,
disconnect power before servicing.

Service error codes table. Columns: error code SSD, error code non-SSD, error,
causes, what to check.

Error code E1 ("QUICK" indicator flashes). Error: WATER INLET FAILURE.
Causes: during the water inlet step, if the flow meter can't detect a correct
fill after 4 minutes, E1 will be displayed.
What to check: 1. Check the water supply. 2. Check the inlet valve.
3. Check the inlet. 4. Check the flow meter. 5. Check the pressure switch.
6. Check the drain. 7. Check the control.

Error code E3 ("QUICK" & "GLASS" indicators flash). Error: HEATER FAILURE.
Causes: when the temperature doesn't reach the correct value after 90 minutes,
E3 will be displayed.
What to check: 1. Check the heater. 2. Check the thermistor. 3. Check the control.

Error code E4 ("LIGHT" indicator flashes). Error: OVERFLOW.
Causes: if water flows into the base and activates the overflow switch, the
dishwasher will display E4.
What to check: 1. Check the use of the detergent. 2. Check whether the
appliance is level. 3. Check the overflow switch. 4. Check the drain pump.
5. Check the amount of water in sump. 6. Locate leak and repair.

Error code E6 ("LIGHT" & "GLASS" indicators flash). Error: NTC OPEN CIRCUIT
FAILURE. Causes: temperature sensor open circuit, E6 will be displayed.
What to check: 1. Check the inlet water temperature. 2. Check the thermistor.
3. Check the control.

Error code E7 ("LIGHT, GLASS & QUICK" indicators flash). Error: NTC SHORT
CIRCUIT FAILURE. Causes: temperature sensor short circuit, E7 will be displayed.
What to check: 1. Check the inlet water temperature. 2. Check the thermistor.
3. Check the control.

Failure solutions: if an alarm occurs, the machine will enter a failure
solving process. The buzzer sounds once per second for 30 seconds. The error
code will be displayed. During that time, the drain pump will turn on for
2 minutes. SSD: Seven Segment Display (not available on 24" front display
dishwashers).

## Page 19 - Troubleshooting Guide

For service technician use only.
Troubleshooting guide table. Columns: description, potential causes, check.

Description: WON'T RUN OR POWER UP ("DEAD" KEYPAD/CONSOLE). No operation.
No keypad response. No LEDs or display.
Potential cause: no power to unit or bad connection.
Check: check fuses, circuit breakers and junction box connections.
Potential cause: loose connections in dishwasher power up circuit or between
keypad(s) and control.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check resistance of all
connections in power up circuit to control. Check connections between
keypad(s) and control.
Potential cause: door switch not making contact (faulty door latch assembly,
faulty door switch).
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Measure resistance of door
switch contacts while checking mechanical operation of latch assembly.
Confirm switches not loose from assembly.
Potential cause: multiple open or shorted circuits in keypad.
Check: see "checking keypad operation".
Potential cause: faulty control.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check/replace control.

Description: WON'T RUN AND LED FOR START/RESUME KEY IS DEAD.
Potential cause: by design, if the door is opened or power is interrupted
during a cycle, the user must press the Start/Resume key to resume operation.
Check: instruct customer, refer to use and care guide.
Potential cause: the door must be latched for the Start/Resume key to work.
Check: confirm door is closed properly before pressing Start/Resume key.
Potential cause: door switch not making contact (faulty or loose door latch
assembly, which can be aggravated by high door closure force; faulty door
switch with high resistance; faulty control does not detect door closed).
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Measure resistance of door
switch contacts while checking mechanical operation of latch assembly. Check
strike plate and door closure. 3. Check/replace control.
Potential cause: Start/Resume key not responding.
Check: see "one or more keys won't respond" problem below.

For service technician use only.

Description: CONTROL LOCK WON'T ACCEPT KEY PRESSES, CONTROL LOCK LED ON.
Potential cause: intermittent open or short of Heated Dry key or circuit in
keypad or keypad connection.
Check: see "checking keypad operation".

Description: ONE OR MORE KEYS WON'T RESPOND.
Potential cause: open key or LED circuit(s) on the keypad, or open circuits on
the control to the keys and LEDs.
Check: see "checking keypad operation".
Potential cause: door switch not making contact.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Measure resistance of door
switch contacts while checking mechanical operation of latch assembly. Check
strike plate and door closure. 3. Check/replace control.

Description: WILL NOT FILL OR LOW WATER.
Potential cause: low water pressure or no water to dishwasher.
Check: verify water is turned on and supply line adequate. Correct
installation as necessary.
Potential cause: loose connection to dishwasher fill valve, or in the valve
circuit or in fill valve solenoid.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check resistances of fill
valve solenoid and all connections in the fill circuit.
Potential cause: drain loop detached from tub and/or improper drain connection.
Check: check for water siphoning out of the unit. Allow dishwasher to complete
a normal fill, drain for 5-10 seconds by pressing Cancel/Drain, open door and
confirm water does not siphon out of unit. If it does, confirm drain loop is
attached to side of dishwasher and drain hose is connected to a drain at least
50.8 cm (20 inches) off the floor.
Potential cause: inlet screen on fill valve plugged.
Check: disconnect water line to fill valve and inspect inlet for obstruction.
Potential cause: faulty fill valve drive circuit on the control.
Check: check operation of fill valve by control during diagnostics.
Potential cause: customer misunderstands proper water level.
Check: customer instruct. Normal water level is just above filter screen.

Description: WASH PUMP WILL NOT PUMP.
Potential cause: pump motor seized up (will not turn).
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power.
Potential cause: pump motor windings open or loose/open connection in wash
pump circuit.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check resistance of pump
motor windings and all connections in the wash circuit including the pump
motor capacitor.

## Page 21 - Troubleshooting Guide (continued)

For service technician use only.

Description: WASH PUMP WILL NOT PUMP (continued).
Potential cause: door switch not making contact consistently (faulty or sloppy
door latch assembly, faulty door switch with high resistance).
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Measure resistance of door
switch contacts while checking mechanical operation of latch assembly.
Potential cause: faulty motor start capacitor.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Disconnect capacitor leads
and confirm capacitance is as labeled on part or check resistance for open
circuit across the terminals. Replace if open.
Potential cause: excessive suds in pump due to using wrong or too much
detergent or rinse aid.
Check: 1. Instruct customer; refer to use and care manual. 2. Repeatedly fill
and cancel/drain to remove suds. 3. Check dispenser in diagnostics and rinse
aid setting.
Potential cause: faulty wash pump drive circuit on the control.
Check: check operation of wash pump motor during diagnostics.

Description: WILL NOT DRAIN OR EXCESS WATER LEFT IN UNIT.
Potential cause: obstructed drain hose or path.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check for blockages from
sump check valve to customer's plumbing. Potential items: plugged garbage
disposer or plug not knocked out, blocked/stuck sump or drain loop check
valve, plugged hoses.
Potential cause: open winding on drain pump motor or loose/open connection in
drain motor circuit.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check the resistances of
drain motor windings and all connections in drain circuit.
Potential cause: drain pump impeller fractured.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Remove drain pump and
check impeller by pulling and rotating. If stripped - replace.
Potential cause: diameter of drain hose too small.
Check: check/install proper drain hose.
Potential cause: drain loop check valve not sealing and customer
disposer/waste line partially or fully plugged.
Check: 1. Disconnect drain hose at plumbing connection. 2. Elevate hose above
dishwasher and fill with water. If water flows into dishwasher, replace entire
drain loop (install as high as possible and attach to underside of counter top
if possible).
Potential cause: faulty drain motor drive circuit on control.
Check: check operation of drain motor during diagnostics.

For service technician use only.

Description: DETERGENT NOT DISPENSING.
Potential cause: item in lower rack blocked lid or blocked spray of water to
dispenser.
Check: instruct customer on proper dish loading.
Potential cause: mechanical binding of dispenser lid.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check/replace dispenser.
Potential cause: lid latch binding due to excess detergent in mechanism.
Check: instruct customer on proper dispenser filling.
Potential cause: open coil on dispenser solenoid or loose/open connection in
dispenser circuit.
Check: 1. Unplug dishwasher or disconnect power. 2. Check resistance of
dispenser coil and all connections in dispenser circuit.
Potential cause: faulty dispenser drive circuit on control.
Check: check operation of dispenser during diagnostics.

Description: POOR WASH.
Potential cause: plugged or damaged screens.
Check: inspect following 3 screens: remove lower spray arm assembly and
inspect foreign object and chopper assembly screens. Clean/replace as needed.
Potential cause: spray arms not rotating.
Check: check for free and proper arm rotation by operating dishwasher and
opening door to see whether arms remain in the same position.
Potential cause: low inlet water temperature or hooked up to the cold water
line.
Check: confirm temperature at sink (recommend 49 C / 120 F). Instruct customer
to run water at sink before running dishwasher.

Description: FILM ON GLASSES AND/OR DISHES.
Potential cause: hard water leaving film on dishes.
Check: check water hardness. If hard, instruct customer to use maximum
detergent. Also recommend the high temp option.
Potential cause: drain loop check valve not sealing.
Check: disconnect drain hose at plumbing connection. Elevate hose above
dishwasher and fill with water. If water flows into dishwasher, replace entire
drain loop.

Description: POOR DRY.
Potential cause: customer not using rinse aid and/or heated dry.
Check: recommend use of rinse aid and heated dry. Instruct customer how to
fill and monitor rinse aid.
Potential cause: rinse aid dispenser not dispensing.
Check: see last three items under "detergent not dispensing".

## Page 23 - Customer Troubleshooting Guide

For service technician use only.
Customer troubleshooting guide (from use & care guide).

Customer complaint: DISHWASHER DOES NOT RUN OR STOPS DURING A CYCLE.
Recommended solutions: it is normal for certain cycles to repeatedly pause for
several seconds during the main wash. Is the door closed tightly and latched?
Is the right cycle selected? Is there power to the dishwasher? Has a household
fuse blown or has a circuit breaker tripped? Replace the fuse or reset the
circuit breaker. Is the water shut-off valve (if installed) turned on?

Customer complaint: DISHES DO NOT DRY COMPLETELY.
Recommended solutions: use of rinse aid along with the heated dry option is
needed for proper drying. Proper loading of items can affect drying. Glasses
and cups with concave bottoms hold water. Unload the lower rack first.

Customer complaint: NOISE.
Recommended solutions: surging sounds can occur periodically throughout the
cycle while the dishwasher is draining. Normal water valve hissing may be
heard periodically. Be sure the filters are properly installed. A thumping
sound may be heard if items interfere with the wash arms. Readjust the dishes
and resume the cycle.

Customer complaint: SPOTTING/FILMING.
Recommended solutions: liquid rinse aid is necessary for drying and to reduce
spotting. Use the correct amount of detergent. Be sure the incoming water
temperature is set at 120 F (49 C). Try using the Hi Temp wash option. To
remove spotting, run a vinegar rinse through the dishwasher.
