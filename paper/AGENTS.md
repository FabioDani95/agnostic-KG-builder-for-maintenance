# Paper working instructions

At the beginning of every task touching this folder, read `WRITING_GUIDELINES.md`
and `STATUS.md`. Read `evaluation/PROTOCOL.md` before changing experiments, metrics
or numerical claims. Update `STATUS.md` when evidence or manuscript readiness changes.

Write manuscript prose in academic British English. Coordination documents may
be Italian. Use `references.bib` citation keys and verify the source supports the
specific claim. Keep measured results, hypotheses, implementation facts and
planned experiments visibly separate. Do not invent missing results or references.

Use `WORKPLAN.md` to track deliverables, dependencies and completion criteria.
Do not introduce dates, duration estimates, weekly plans or deadlines into the
work plan unless the user asks. Dates identifying sources, versions or runs are
appropriate metadata.

`reference_material/` contains archival material, not active instructions.
The adapted `WRITING_GUIDELINES.md` governs paper writing. Do not restore EU
proposal requirements, a two-page limit, TRLs or funding-call language.
