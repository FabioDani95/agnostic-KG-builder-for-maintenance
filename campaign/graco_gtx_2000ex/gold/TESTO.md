[p8.b2] Troubleshooting
[p8.b3] Before performing any Troubleshooting procedures, follow Pressure Relief procedure on page 6.
[p8.t1.r1] TABLE COLUMNS: PROBLEM | CAUSE | SOLUTION
[p8.t1.r2] PROBLEM: No material output from pump | CAUSE: Not enough air pressure to pump | SOLUTION: Shut off air at the gun, and increase air pressure to the pump to maximum. Turn regulator clockwise to increase.
[p8.t1.r3] PROBLEM (same as row above): No material output from pump | CAUSE: Material too thick | SOLUTION: Thin the material. Material must be mixed thoroughly to a consistency that immediately folds back in as you draw your finger through the surface of the material.
[p8.t1.r4] PROBLEM (same as row above): No material output from pump | CAUSE: Damaged checkballs or seats | SOLUTION: Replace checkballs or seats.
[p8.t1.r5] PROBLEM (same as row above): No material output from pump | CAUSE: Plugged gun or nozzle | SOLUTION: Relieve Pressure, page 6. Remove gun from material hose, and cycle pump. NOTE: A plugged gun or nozzle may cause the hose and pump to plug. If necessary, flush hose and pump with clean water before cycling material through the hose with the gun removed.
[p8.t1.r6] PROBLEM (same as row above): No material output from pump | CAUSE: Plugged hose, or hose too small | SOLUTION: Relieve pressure, page 6. Flush hose with clean water, or try a 1-1/4 in. hose.
[p8.t1.r7] PROBLEM: Pump stops pumping | CAUSE: Stalled pump | SOLUTION: Shut down system. Relieve Pressure, page 6. Restart.
[p8.t1.r8] PROBLEM (same as row above): Pump stops pumping | CAUSE (same as row above): Stalled pump | SOLUTION: Contact an authorized Graco service center.
[p8.t1.r9] PROBLEM (same as row above): Pump stops pumping | CAUSE: Pump in need of repair | SOLUTION: See texture pump instruction manual 308479.
[p8.t1.r10] PROBLEM: Pulsing or surging material | CAUSE: Triggering too fast | SOLUTION: Slowly squeeze trigger to fully open position while moving gun quickly in a circular motion.
[p8.t1.r11] PROBLEM: Speed of application too slow | CAUSE: Not enough air pressure to pump | SOLUTION: Shut off air at the gun, and increase air pressure to the pump to maximum. Turn regulator clockwise to increase.
[p8.t1.r12] PROBLEM (same as row above): Speed of application too slow | CAUSE: Material too thick | SOLUTION: Material must be mixed thoroughly to a consistency that immediately folds back in as you draw your finger through the surface of the material.
[p8.t1.r13] PROBLEM (same as row above): Speed of application too slow | CAUSE: Nozzle too small | SOLUTION: Increase nozzle size.
[p8.t1.r14] PROBLEM (same as row above): Speed of application too slow | CAUSE: Hose plugged or too small. | SOLUTION: Relieve Pressure, page 6. Clean hose, or try a 1-1/4 in. hose.
[p8.t1.r15] PROBLEM (same as row above): Speed of application too slow | CAUSE: Pump in need of repair | SOLUTION: See texture pump instruction manual 308479.
